TESTIMONY OF RISK MANAGER SHAW
Cross-Examination - Exxon Defense Counsel

Q: You managed risk for Exxon's coastal operations?
A: Yes, I was the senior risk manager for fifteen years. I oversaw safety protocols and emergency response planning.

Q: How did you respond to the incident?
A: We activated our emergency response plan within two hours. We deployed containment booms, hired third-party cleaners, and coordinated with state and federal agencies.

Q: Were protocols followed?
A: We followed all established safety protocols. The equipment was inspected and certified. The failure was due to an unexpected storm that exceeded design specifications.

Q: Could contamination have other sources?
A: Yes. Natural seeps, agricultural runoff, and shipping traffic all contribute to hydrocarbon levels. The plaintiffs haven't ruled out these alternative sources.

Q: How do you respond to the damages claims?
A: The plaintiffs' estimates ignore baseline economic conditions and natural seasonal variations. Many claimed losses overlap with normal market fluctuations that have nothing to do with our incident.
