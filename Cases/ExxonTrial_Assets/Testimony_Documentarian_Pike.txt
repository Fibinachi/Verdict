TESTIMONY OF DOCUMENTARIAN PIKE
Direct Examination - Plaintiffs' Counsel

Q: You reviewed Exxon's internal documents?
A: Yes, I analyzed thousands of pages of internal communications, safety reports, and risk assessments from 2015-2020.

Q: What did you find about warnings?
A: There were multiple internal alerts about aging infrastructure and potential failure points. Safety recommendations were documented but not always implemented.

Q: How does this relate to the spill?
A: The documents show the company was aware of risks but prioritized cost savings. Internal emails reveal discussions about "acceptable risk levels" that proved too high.

Q: What about mitigation efforts?
A: The company had contingency plans but didn't activate them promptly. Delayed response allowed the spill to spread beyond manageable limits.

Q: Does this show disregard?
A: It shows a pattern of cost-cutting that increased risk. When safety recommendations were ignored and warning signs were dismissed, the company acted with disregard for potential consequences.
