TESTIMONY OF ECONOMIST DR. DANA BROOKS
Cross-Examination - Exxon Defense Counsel

Q: You specialize in environmental economics?
A: Yes, I've studied the economic impacts of environmental incidents for twenty years.

Q: How should damages be calculated?
A: Economic harm must be modeled with baseline controls. You need to account for normal seasonal fluctuations and unrelated market trends that affect tourism independently.

Q: What about the plaintiff's calculations?
A: They double-count losses by including economic factors that would have occurred anyway. A recession, increased competition, and changing travel patterns all affected the region during the relevant period.

Q: Can you quantify alternative factors?
A: Yes. Tourism data shows a 15% decline due to economic conditions and a 10% decline due to increased competition from other destinations. These factors alone could explain much of the claimed losses.

Q: Do you dispute environmental harm?
A: I don't dispute that harm occurred. I dispute that all economic damage is attributable to the spill. The plaintiffs haven't separated spill-related losses from other causal factors.
