TESTIMONY OF BUSINESS OWNER JAMES
Direct Examination - Plaintiffs' Counsel

Q: You own the Marina Bay Resort?
A: Yes, I've owned it for twelve years. It's been in my family for generations.

Q: How did the spill affect your business?
A: Tourism collapsed overnight. We went from 200 visitors per day during peak season to fewer than 20. Our restaurant, gift shop, and charter services all saw revenue drops of 80-90%.

Q: What are your damages?
A: I've documented lost revenue of $1.5 million over three tourist seasons. I also invested in remediation efforts - $200,000 in cleanup and restoration that should have been unnecessary.

Q: Did you try to mitigate losses?
A: I reduced prices, offered specials, and partnered with local hotels. But the reputation damage was severe. People associated the area with contamination.

Q: How has your community suffered?
A: Dozens of businesses closed. Hundreds of jobs disappeared. The local tax base eroded. This isn't just my personal loss - it's a community catastrophe.
