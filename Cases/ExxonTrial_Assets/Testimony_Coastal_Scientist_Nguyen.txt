TESTIMONY OF COASTAL SCIENTIST DR. NGYN
Direct Examination - Plaintiffs' Counsel

Q: Dr. Ngyn, your research focus?
A: Marine ecology and environmental impact assessment. I've studied coastal ecosystems for fifteen years.

Q: What did your sampling reveal?
A: Water and sediment samples from the affected area show elevated hydrocarbon levels consistent with the chemical signature of the spill. Tissue samples from local wildlife indicate bioaccumulation.

Q: How do you establish causation?
A: The temporal proximity between the spill and observed impacts, combined with the unique chemical fingerprint, supports a causal link. While natural variability exists, the magnitude of change exceeds normal fluctuations.

Q: What's the extent of ecological harm?
A: We documented a 40% decline in local fish populations, coral bleaching, and contamination of shellfish beds. Recovery is underway but remains incomplete after five years.

Q: Are you certain about industrial causation?
A: The evidence strongly supports it. Alternative sources would need to explain the identical chemical signature and timing, which hasn't been demonstrated.
