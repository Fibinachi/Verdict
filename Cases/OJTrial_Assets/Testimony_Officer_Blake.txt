TESTIMONY OF OFFICER JAMES BLAKE
Cross-Examination - Defense

Q: Officer, your role in this case?
A: I was assigned to process the crime scene and handle evidence collection.

Q: Any concerns about evidence handling?
A: There were some irregularities. The chain of custody forms had gaps - signatures missing, dates unclear. Items were labeled with different numbering systems between the LAPD and the lab.

Q: Did you personally collect all evidence?
A: No. I oversaw the process. There were three officers collecting evidence that night. I documented what I observed, but I can't speak to every detail of every collection.

Q: Were there any contamination issues?
A: The lab reported some samples showed degradation. They attributed it to storage conditions, but I noted the evidence containers didn't match standard protocols.

Q: Do you think evidence could have been mishandled?
A: Based on my experience and the documentation gaps, yes, there were opportunities for mishandling or cross-contamination.
