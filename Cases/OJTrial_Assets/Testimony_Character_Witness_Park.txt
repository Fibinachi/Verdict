TESTIMONY OF CHARACTER WITNESS PARK
Cross-Examination - Defense

Q: You know the defendant personally?
A: Yes, we've been neighbors for eight years. He's a teacher at the local school. I've watched his kids grow up.

Q: What's your opinion of his character?
A: He's one of the most honest, hardworking people I know. Always helping people in the community. He coaches youth basketball and volunteers at the food bank.

Q: Have you ever seen him behave violently?
A: Never. Not once. He's the opposite of violent - he's gentle, protective even.

Q: Would he hurt someone?
A: That's absurd. The defendant is peaceful, religious, family-oriented. Anyone who knows him would say the same thing.

Q: Does character matter in this trial?
A: It matters enormously. People don't suddenly become criminals. The defendant's actions that night would be completely out of character.
