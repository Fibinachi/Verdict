TESTIMONY OF EYEWITNESS MAYA STONE
Direct Examination - Prosecution

Q: Ms. Stone, can you describe what you saw?
A: I was walking home from the store around 9 PM when I heard shouting. I saw a figure near the victim, and later, when police showed me photos, I identified the defendant.

Q: How confident are you in this identification?
A: I've thought about it many times. Human memory isn't perfect, but I remember specific details - the height, the build, the way they moved. I'm confident it was the defendant.

Q: Were there other people around?
A: There was some traffic, but I had a clear view of the area for several seconds. I wasn't wearing glasses that night, which I know affects vision.

Q: Did you notice anything unusual about the lighting?
A: It was streetlit, not bright. I'd say it was moderate lighting. I squinted a bit because of the brightness of someone's car headlights.

Q: Any doubt in your identification?
A: No. I understand the experts say cross-racial identification is harder, but I've lived in this neighborhood for years and I know the defendant is the person I saw.
