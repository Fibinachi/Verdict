TESTIMONY OF LINGUIST EXPERT DR. HART
Cross-Examination - Defense

Q: Dr. Hart, your expertise in linguistics?
A: PhD in Comparative Linguistics, specializing in historical document translation and interpretation.

Q: You've reviewed the prosecution's documents?
A: Yes. Several translations contain ambiguities and potential misinterpretations that change the meaning significantly.

Q: Give me an example.
A: A phrase translated as "we will proceed" could equally be read as "we will withdraw" depending on the original Italian dialect and context. The prosecution chose the more incriminating translation.

Q: Does this affect the case?
A: It undermines the prosecution's documentary evidence foundation. If key documents are misinterpreted, the entire narrative shifts.

Q: What's your conclusion?
A: The translation issues create reasonable doubt about whether the documents support the prosecution's theory at all.
