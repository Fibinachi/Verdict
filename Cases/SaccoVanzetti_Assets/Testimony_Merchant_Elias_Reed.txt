TESTIMONY OF MERCHANT ELIAS REED
Direct Examination - Prosecution

Q: You operate the general store near the factory?
A: Yes, I've owned Reed's General Store for twenty years. I know everyone in town.

Q: What did you observe on the day of the incident?
A: I saw both defendants purchasing supplies - gunpowder, fuses, and materials that could be used for mining operations. They were nervous, asking questions about safety procedures.

Q: How confident are you in your memory?
A: I've thought about this daily for months. The circumstances were unusual - they came in specifically asking about materials, and I remember their faces clearly. I identified them in a lineup without difficulty.

Q: Did anyone pressure you?
A: No pressure whatsoever. I told the truth as I remember it. If anything, I had reasons to be cautious since the case attracted attention.

Q: Why should jurors trust your identification?
A: Because it's accurate. I've lived in this community my entire life. I have no motive to lie.
