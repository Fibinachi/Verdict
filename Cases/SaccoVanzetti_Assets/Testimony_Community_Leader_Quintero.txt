TESTIMONY OF COMMUNITY LEADER PARKER QUINTERO
Cross-Examination - Defense

Q: You lead the Italian-American Community Center?
A: Yes, I've been the director for fifteen years. I know Sacco and Vanzetti and their families personally.

Q: What's your view of this case?
A: The prosecution's case appears driven by political pressure rather than purely reliable facts. These men are respected members of our community.

Q: How does politics factor in?
A: The incident occurred during a period of heightened labor tensions. Some evidence suggests investigators may have been influenced by anti-immigrant sentiment in the community.

Q: Does this matter to the case?
A: It matters enormously. Jurors should consider whether the investigation was fair and impartial or influenced by external pressures.

Q: What about community support?
A: The defendants have broad community support because people believe in their innocence. The case has become symbolic of larger issues with the justice system.
