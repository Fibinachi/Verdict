TESTIMONY OF INSPECTOR HENRI MOREAU
Direct Examination - Prosecution

Q: Inspector, please describe your investigation.
A: I led the investigation into the workplace explosion. We interviewed numerous witnesses and collected documents from the scene.

Q: What did you find regarding the defendants' presence?
A: The evidence places both Sacco and Vanzetti at the factory on the day of the incident. Witness accounts, payroll records, and security footage all corroborate their presence during the critical hours.

Q: How do you respond to concerns about witness reliability?
A: I vetted witness credibility carefully. While some witnesses had reasons to dislike the defendants, their core testimony about presence remains consistent across multiple independent sources.

Q: Was there any coercion in testimony?
A: I explained my observations about timeline inconsistencies to witnesses, but I never pressured anyone to say anything specific. Their accounts were voluntary and consistent.

Q: What's your conclusion?
A: The defendants were present, they had access to the materials, and the circumstances suggest their involvement in the incident.
