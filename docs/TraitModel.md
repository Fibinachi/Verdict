## Key Formulas

RWA: clamp10(5 + bias×3.2×PolW + age_factor + (ethnicityW−0.5)×0.6 + compound×0.5 + implicit×0.3 + noise×1.8)
PolId: clamp11(bias × PolWeight + implicit × 0.2)
Punitiveness: clamp10(5 + bias×2.1×PolW + (religionW−0.5)×1.0 + age_curve×1.2 + implicit×0.4 + noise×1.8)

## Cross-Factor Interactions

Race×Gender: Non-white female −0.15×ethnicityW; male −0.10×ethnicityW (Crenshaw 1989)
Edu×Politics: eduW×polW×0.20 (Stage 2 dynamic, Kahan 2012)
Age×Punitiveness: U-shaped: young (30−age)×0.04; old (age−60)×0.03 (Steiner 2001)
Gender×Religion: (genderW−0.5)×(religionW−0.5)×0.6 (Burt & Albin 1981)
Religion×Politics: (religionW−0.5)×(polW−0.5)×0.4
