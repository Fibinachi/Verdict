Trial Stages

Verdict includes a comprehensive stage management system that allows you to track and control the progress of your case through the legal process.

The Stage Menu

The Stage menu is located in the main menu bar between the File and Case menus. It provides two levels of stage control:

Trial Phases

The top-level trial phases represent the major stages of a legal proceeding:

Discovery

The pre-trial phase where both parties exchange information, gather evidence, and prepare their cases. During this phase, you can submit evidence, interview witnesses, and build your strategy.

Pretrial

The pretrial phase includes motions, jury selection, and final preparations before the trial begins. Use this phase to finalize your evidence list and configure juror profiles.

Trial

The main trial phase where evidence is presented, witnesses testify, and arguments are made. The trial phase contains several sub-phases (court phases) that represent the progression of the trial itself.

Court Phases (Trial Sub-stages)

When the trial phase is active, you can select from the following court phases:

Experimental: Court phase selection is currently an experimental feature. While the UI lets you pick sub-phases, the underlying simulation may still rely primarily on the jury-deliberation behavior until stage-specific logic is completed.

Opening Statements (Experimental)

Both sides present their opening statements to the jury, outlining what they intend to prove during the trial. (Limited implementation)

Witness Testimony (Experimental)

Witnesses are called to testify and present their accounts of events relevant to the case. (Limited implementation)

Cross Examination (Experimental)

The opposing side questions witnesses to challenge their testimony and credibility. (Limited implementation)

Party Statements (Experimental)

The parties themselves (plaintiff/prosecution and defense) may make statements to the court. (Limited implementation)

Closing Arguments (Experimental)

Both sides present their final summaries and arguments to the jury before deliberation. (Limited implementation)

Jury Deliberation (Active)

The jury retires to deliberate and reach a verdict. Jurors' sentiments and biases influence their final decision. (Fully functional)

Dynamic Title Bar

The window title updates dynamically to reflect the current case and stage. The format is:

VERDICT - Plaintiff v. Defendant - (CASE STAGE)

For example: VERDICT - Smith v. Johnson - (Trial - Opening Statements)

The title bar updates automatically when:

  • A new case is loaded or created.
  • The trial phase is changed via the Stage menu.
  • The court phase is changed during the trial.
Tip: Use the Stage menu to track your progress through the case. The current stage is always visible in the window title bar for quick reference.
